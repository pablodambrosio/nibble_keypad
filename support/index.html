Confirm Factory Reset?

This operation cannot be undone

Executing a factory reset will:

  • Revert all 4 keys to factory assignments (A, B, C, D)
  • Reset the power management profile to Normal Mode
  • Erase the kb_cfg persistent NVS namespace
  • Forget all paired host devices (purges NimBLE bonding keys)
  • Disconnect this session and restart the ESP32-C3
